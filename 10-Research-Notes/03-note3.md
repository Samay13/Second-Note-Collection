# Title



Functional Dependency versus Psychological Dependency



# Quotation



"We distinguish between two forms of dependency: functional dependency, which refers to reliance on social media to accomplish goals and needs, and psychological dependency, which refers to habitual or com-pulsive forms of engagement characterized by diminished control."(Schokkenbroek et al. 2026, 2).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it breaks down reliance on social media into two different types. Functional dependency means using a platform because they have become the normal way to solve everyday things like school, work, and social-life. Psychological dependency is more about habits, feeling like it's out of control, and having an urge. The authors use this distinction to explain why people use social media so much without labeling anything as an addiction. This relates to students relying on AI tools by viewing that students might use AI for practical tasks, but can also slowly turn into a habit that's extremely tough to break. 



# Subject Terms
