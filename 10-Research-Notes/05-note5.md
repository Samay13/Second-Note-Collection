# Title



The Dual-Path Framework for Human/AI Collaboration



# Quotation



"Unlike existing studies that treat AI effects separately, the proposed model integrates both learning enhancement and dependency mechanisms into a unified structure. Specifically, we formalize cognitive support and cognitive offloading as computational variables and construct two parallel pathways to model their influence on learning outcomes."(Sun et al. 2026, 17).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it introduces a full framework where instead of looking at the positive and the negative sides of AI use separately, it looks at both sides at the same time. Instead of just stating that AI is either good or bad, the authors break it down into two parallel paths. Cognitive support is the first one where it talks about helping the students learn better. The second one is cognitive loading where it talks about the risks that are making students too dependent. This dual-path model shows that it works against both sides. This shows that there is a structured way to explain how students using AI assistants can build their skills as well as having a drop in independent problem-solving at the same time.
