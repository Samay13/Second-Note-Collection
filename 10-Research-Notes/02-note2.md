# Title



How Anxiety and Trust Change the Usage of AI



# Quotation



"Moderation analysis showed that anxiety weakened the positive impact of cognition on behavior, while trust amplified this relationship."(Zheng, Hu, and Wang 2025, 233).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it shows proof on how a student's understanding or confidence in AI isn't enough on its own to be able to predict how much the student will use AI. Instead, their emotions play a significant role. When students feel anxiety, it stops them relying on AI too much. On the other side, when students feel trust, it boosts their confidence greatly and results in them relying on AI even more than they did before. This explains why some students develop a caution while others develop a growing dependence.



# Subject Terms



Anxiety, Trust, AI Dependence, Student Behavior
