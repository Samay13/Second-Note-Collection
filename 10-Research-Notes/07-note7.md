# Title



The Impact of AI Dependence



# Quotation



"The findings suggest that emotional and behavioral dependence on generative AI may be associated with lower levels of perceived innovative thinking, possibly by reducing independent cognitive engagement."(Wu and Zhang 2026, 850).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it goes over the specific risk that some forms of reliance, like attachment and habits, challenges the student's abilities. Instead of using AI as a tool for quick answers, whenever students become over-dependent or spend too much time using it, their independent working, problem-solving, and efforts decline. This study explains how the students are looking for easy answers that will also replace their actual effort that was put in. This is important because it connects how students relying on AI too heavily will drop their originality, creativity, and critical thinking throughout their academic careers.



# Subject Terms



Emotional/Behavioral Dependence, Innovative Thinking, Student Abilities
