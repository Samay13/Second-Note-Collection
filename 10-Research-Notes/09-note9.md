# Title



Buckpassing and How it Shapes AI Reliance



# Quotation



"Conversely, the higher a participant’s tendency for buckpassing, the more likely they were to choose to see ChatGPT’s decisions, the less time they spent looking at its explanations for the decision, and the higher their self-reported reliance on ChatGPT."(Mei et al. 2026, 2-3).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it shows that people who attempt to avoid making tough decisions, or buckpassers, typically turn to AI for answers more often and also spend less time actually understanding the reasoning behind the answers that are given by the AI assistants. Instead of taking a second to try and check whether the answer that AI gave is entirely correct, they lean on it and rely on it heavily and then simply move onto the next thing. This pattern shows that people are more likely to accept incorrect AI responses without questioning their result. This helps explain why students who want a quick way out of their tough work can easily fall into over-reliance on AI assistance without receiving any actual understanding on that work.
