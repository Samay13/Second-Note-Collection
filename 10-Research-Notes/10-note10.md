# Title



The Higher Buckpassing Rate Amongst Younger Students



# Quotation



"Consistent with prior findings \[29], our study suggests that buckpassing tendency is more prevalent among younger people (especially individuals under 18) and people with high school education or below. This warrants greater intervention to guide these populations in their usage of AI systems."(Mei et al. 2026, 18).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it shows how people tend to build the habit of handing off tough or difficult tasks off to others at a younger age and education. Their study found that younger people and those who have a high school education or less are more likely to buckpass. This means that they turn to AI assistants more often than they should when even facing simple choices or problems. This shows that there needs to be guidance for these students at a younger age to make sure that these habits don't grow or begin with in the start.
