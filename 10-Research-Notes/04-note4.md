# Title



How Platforms and Habits Make Vulnerability



# Quotation



"When compulsive use narrows the repertoire of channels through which needs are met, social media platforms increasingly become the default means of satisfying both functional and psychological demands."(Schokkenbroek et al. 2026, 4).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it explains how users become trapped into relying more on digital platforms. When people begin to use only one platform for everything, such as news, entertainment, or connecting with others, they stop looking for other ways to be able to get that same information. After some time, that platform becomes the default answer for practical tasks and comfort. This connects to AI reliance with students by connecting their functional and psychological needs. As students rely more and more on AI, they will stop looking for other ways to solve their problems. This also greatly weakens their independent problem-solving skills and continues to build up their dependence on AI as well.
