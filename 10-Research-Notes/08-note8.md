# Title



Random Forest Model and the Limitations on its Explanatory Power



# Quotation



"Furthermore, the model’s coefficient of determination (R2)is 0.1942, indicating that the AI dependence features and control variables explain approximately 19.42% of the variance in college students’ innovative thinking abilities. This result suggests that while the Random Forest model can capture the nonlinear relationship between AI dependence and innovative thinking to some extent, its overall explanatory power is relatively limited."(Wu and Zhang 2026, 853).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it provides real, measurable stats for how much AI reliance actually affects the student's innovative thinking abilities. The R2 value shows that even though AI dependence has a connection to lower innovation numbers, it only take account for about 19.4% of the variation. This means that innovation is shaped by many other unmeasurable things like motivation and educational backgrounds. Dependence on AI is a giant factor in hurting a student's thinking ability, but it also works alongside many other influences.
