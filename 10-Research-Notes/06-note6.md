# Title



Self-Regulation and Task Complexity



# Quotation



"Self-regulation plays acritical role, as learners with higher self-regulation are more likely to use AI as a supportive tool rather than a substitute for thinking. Task complexity also influences AI use, with more complex tasks benefiting from AI support and simpler tasks potentially encouraging over-reliance."(Sun et al. 2026, 18).



# Commentary



This quote is important because it shows the specific conditions that determines whether a student gains from using AI or becomes dependent on it. It explains that learners with a higher self-regulation are typically more resistant to the dependence. Their strong self-control helps them use AI as a useful assistant, while the opposite with weaker self-control has AI turn into a replacement for their own mind. Relying on AI for simple tasks as well can easily turn into overreliance and the building of bad habits. This connects to student behavior by showing that the danger of relying on AI isn't simple, but depends greatly on the student's self-control and the type of coursework they're working on.
